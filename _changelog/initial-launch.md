## Added

- Public web application at https://spectra.rombo.ai
- Self-service authentication via Auth0 (email, passkey, Google) at `/login`
- Marketing homepage describing compound identification from 1H and 13C data
- Named source families: Bruker, JEOL, Varian, and JCAMP-DX
- Four-step workflow: load, retrieve, validate against physics, review evidence

## Improved

- Product naming on the live site is **Spectra by ROMBO AI** (auth screens also use Spectra AI)

## Fixed

- Nothing additional is claimed beyond the live deployment dated 14 September 2026 (build `20260914T125550Z`)

## Known limitations

- Public alpha (`0.6.0-alpha`)
- No published product accuracy KPI
- No published regulatory validation package
- Broader format matrices are not documented
