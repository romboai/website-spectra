Use these guides to prepare NMR data, work through an analysis, and review the evidence behind a candidate structure. Spectra ranks and explains; the chemist decides. A ranking is not confirmation of molecular identity.

## Your first analysis

1. **Prepare one sample.** Gather its spectra and record the nucleus, solvent and spectrometer frequency where known. Upload only data you are authorized to share.
2. **Review each step.** Check Input, Preprocessing and Peak picking before authorizing the next stage. Processed spectra and imported peak lists can enter further along the workflow.
3. **Evaluate the evidence.** Review the candidates and unexplained signals, not just the top rank. If no single structure is defensible, use the result to decide what additional evidence to collect.

Follow [getting started](/docs/getting-started/) for the walkthrough, then [running an analysis](/docs/running-an-analysis/) for the review checklist.
